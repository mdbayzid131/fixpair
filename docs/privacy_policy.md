# Privacy Policy for Fixpair

**Last Updated: August 15, 2026**

Fixpair ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and our related services. 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App depends on the features and services you use:

### Personal Data
* **Registration Information:** Name, email address, date of birth (users must be at least 18 years old to register), and password.
* **Profile Information:** Profile pictures, descriptions, areas of expertise, and credentials (for consultants).

### Real-Time Communication Data (Video & Audio)
* Our App provides live video and audio consultations powered by Agora RTC.
* **Crucial Note:** All live video and audio communications are streamed in real-time and end-to-end encrypted. We **do not record, store, or monitor** the audio or video content of your consultations on our servers.

### Financial and Payment Data
* Payment processing is securely handled by **Stripe**.
* When you add a payment method (e.g., credit card), the financial data is sent directly to Stripe. Fixpair **does not store** your full credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers. We only store payment tokens and card metadata (e.g., last 4 digits) returned by Stripe to manage your billing.

### Device and Usage Data
* IP address, device type, operating system, push notification tokens (for sending call and booking notifications via Apple APNs / Google FCM), and App usage statistics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
* Create and manage your account.
* Connect clients with verified consultants.
* Facilitate real-time video/audio calls and scheduling.
* Process payments, platform fees, and issue refunds via Stripe.
* Send push notifications regarding bookings, incoming calls, and updates.
* Comply with legal obligations under the GDPR (DSGVO) and other laws.

---

## 3. Data Transfer and Third-Party Services

We share your data with trusted third-party providers who perform services for us, including:
* **Firebase (Google):** For user authentication, cloud database, and push notifications.
* **Stripe:** For secure payment processing and invoicing.
* **Agora:** For hosting real-time audio and video sessions.

All of our partners comply with standard security and privacy regulations (including GDPR).

---

## 4. Your GDPR/DSGVO Rights (For EU/Germany Users)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under the GDPR:
* **Right to Access:** You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
* **Right to Rectification:** You can update or correct your personal data.
* **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** You can request the deletion of your account and personal data.
* **Right to Portability:** You can request to export your data.
* **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at **support@fixpair.de**.

## 1. Erhebung Ihrer Daten

Wir erheben Informationen über Sie auf verschiedene Weise. Die von uns erhobenen Daten hängen von den Funktionen und Diensten ab, die Sie nutzen:

### Personenbezogene Daten
* **Registrierungsdaten:** Name, E-Mail-Adresse, Geburtsdatum (Nutzer müssen mindestens 18 Jahre alt sein) und Passwort.
* **Profildaten:** Profilbilder, Beschreibungen, Fachgebiete und Qualifikationsnachweise (für Berater).

### Echtzeit-Kommunikationsdaten (Video & Audio)
* Unsere App bietet Live-Video- und Audio-Konsultationen über Agora RTC an.
* **Wichtiger Hinweis:** Alle Live-Video- und Audio-Übertragungen erfolgen in Echtzeit und sind Ende-zu-Ende verschlüsselt. Wir **zeichnen keine Video- oder Audioinhalte auf, speichern sie nicht und überwachen sie nicht** auf unseren Servern.

### Finanz- und Zahlungsdaten
* Die Zahlungsabwicklung erfolgt sicher über den Zahlungsdienstleister **Stripe**.
* Wenn Sie eine Zahlungsmethode hinzufügen (z. B. Kreditkarte), werden diese Daten direkt an Stripe gesendet. Fixpair **speichert keine** vollständigen Kreditkartennummern oder CVV-Codes auf unseren Servern. Wir speichern lediglich Zahlungstoken und Karten-Metadaten (z. B. die letzten 4 Ziffern), die von Stripe zur Verwaltung Ihrer Abrechnung zurückgegeben werden.

### Geräte- und Nutzungsdaten
* IP-Adresse, Gerätetyp, Betriebssystem, Push-Benachrichtigungs-Token (zum Senden von Anrufs- und Buchungsbenachrichtigungen über Apple APNs / Google FCM) und App-Nutzungsstatistiken.
